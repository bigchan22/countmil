\begin{abstract}
Aggregate-supervised multiple-instance learning observes a label for each bag but not the labels of individual instances.
Exact likelihoods are known for standard Bernoulli count labels, but many aggregate labels involve more general finite supports, including signed counts with cancellation and integer-valued digit sums.
We introduce FS-Conv, a backbone-agnostic finite-support convolutional likelihood layer that maps per-instance contribution PMFs to the exact model-implied aggregate distribution by discrete convolution.
FS-Conv strictly generalizes the exact Bernoulli count likelihood underlying recent count-based weak-supervision methods: the $\{0,1\}$ support recovers ordinary count loss, while signed and integer-valued supports yield exact likelihoods for cancellation and digit-sum supervision.
Across aggregate-supervised prediction tasks, FS-Conv improves hidden-instance recovery most clearly when the observed aggregate is poorly captured by a mean or proportion alone.
\end{abstract}
